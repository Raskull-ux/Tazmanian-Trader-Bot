# Flipping the six

Setup (knowable at 9:30): yesterday closed on daily TD sell count 5, today opens above yesterday's close. Outcome: the low tags yesterday's close by 12:00 ET, and by the close. Earnings days left out. 75 of his names, 2016 to Oct 14 2026 (5m bars on data_ext).

## Answer

TSLA: flip-the-six days hit the prior close by noon 67% of the time (n 79) vs 61% on every other gap-up day (n 1382); by the close 68% vs 66%.
All his names: by noon 60% (n 5,014) vs 59% (n 91,869); by the close 66% vs 64%.
**Did not hold** as an edge: the count-5 gap-up is not meaningfully more likely to tag the prior close than any other gap-up (bar: 5+ points better by noon in both halves). Most gap-ups tag the prior close anyway; that is the base rate he is seeing.

TSLA alone in 2022-26 is the exception: 76% by noon vs 60% (n 33), and 76% closed red vs 49%. In 2016-21 TSLA showed nothing (61% vs 62%). A recent TSLA-only lean on 33 days, not proof. The gap size matters far more than the count: small gaps tag the prior close 77% of the time either way, 2%+ gaps only about 22%.
