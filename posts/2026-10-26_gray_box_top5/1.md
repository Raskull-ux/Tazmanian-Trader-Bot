📦 **What's a gray box?**
A gap between two daily candle BODIES. Wicks don't count. If a later candle body covers part of it, that part is gone. What's left is the box. Price tends to stall and turn when it runs back into one. 🧱

🎯 **How every setup works (same steps, every time)**
1️⃣ **ARM** ⏳ Price has to make a real move first, AWAY from the box. No arm = no trade. Period.
2️⃣ **TRIGGER** 🚨 After it's armed, price runs back into the box. That tag is the entry.
3️⃣ **STOP** 🛑 Set the second you get in. It doesn't move further away. Ever.
4️⃣ **HALF** 💰 Sell half on the first small move your way. Then move your stop to your entry. Now the rest is a free ride. 🆓
5️⃣ **RUNNER** 🏃 The other half goes for the runner target, or you're out by the close.
