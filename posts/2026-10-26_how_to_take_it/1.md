🌪️ **HOW TO TAKE TODAY'S GRAY BOX TRADES** 🌪️
Simple version. Every trade is 2 steps. 👇

**STEP 1: WAIT FOR THE MOVE** ⏳
Price has to travel to the "First" level before anything happens. No move = no trade. 🚫

**STEP 2: ENTER AT THE TRIGGER** 🎯
After it hits "First", price has to come back to the "Enter" level. That's your entry. 💥

**THEN MANAGE IT** 🧠
🛑 Stop = out. No moving it. No hoping.
💰 Hits "Take half" = sell half, move your stop to your entry. Now you can't lose on it.
🚀 Rest rides to "Runner" or you're out by the close. ⏰

**RULES** ⚠️
❌ If it gaps or blows past "Enter" before the move happens = SKIP.
❌ 2 losses = done for the day.
📅 Options a week or more out. Contracts under $500.
