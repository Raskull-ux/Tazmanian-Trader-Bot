## 2. Trading rules (current)

1. No standalone 0DTE. 0DTE only as an add to a longer-dated position on the same thesis that's already green.
2. Add only to a green position, never to a red one.
3. 2 losing trades = done for the day.
4. RSI 80/20 cutoffs. Refinement: 80 is early, 83 to 85 elevated, 90+ is the real extreme.
5. Contracts: sweet spot $100 to $200; cap under $500 (also said under $300 for the room lane; not settled).
6. Expirations a week or more out for 1 to 5 day holds (prefers 1 to 2 weeks max).
7. Sell half at 2x, let the rest run. He's also fine taking ~50% wins (~30% on longer-dated). He targets stock levels, not option percentages.
8. Next day's setup (ticker, trigger, stop) posted in Discord the night before. At 9:30 the only action is placing that order. Since Oct 27: set orders before the open (limit at Robinhood's estimated contract price at the trigger).
9. Skip the open after a big winner.
10. Balance cap: everything above the base stake gets withdrawn once it settles.
11. Reloads only from gig money. Never bill or rent money. No reload money = alerts that week are "ideas," labeled honestly.
12. Basket trades are tagged separately from single-name setups.
13. Invalidation closes: day trades judged on 5-min closes, swings on daily closes.

**Known leaks (call them out):** impulse entries at the open, looser trades after a win, revenge trades, cutting winners early, mental stops, entering before his own planned level, selling on intrabar wicks before the candle closes, too many tickers in play (plans MSTR, trades QQQ), holding too long and riding winners back to red, get-rich-quick urge.

**Status Oct 28, 2026:** done trading his own account until next month. Meanwhile the room gets max 3 alerts, the most bearish setups.

## 3. Chart settings and conventions

- RSI length 14, exponential averaging (not Wilder). MACD on exponential.
- MAs are SMA except the 8, which is EMA. Colors: 10 black (also 10 SMA green / 10 EMA grey in some charts), 20 blue, 50 yellow, 200 red. ATR 14.
- Extended hours on for every intraday timeframe; daily is regular session.
- Thinkorswim for small timeframes (24 hours shown). TOS 4H bars run 9 to 1 and 1 to 5. TradingView 4H bars run 12 to 4, 4 to 8, 8 to 12. Hourly candles clock-aligned (the 9:00 candle holds the 9:30 open).
- Gray boxes on his charts = unfilled gaps between candle bodies. Wicks don't fill a gap; a solid body must go all the way through. Daily boxes update at each daily close, weekly boxes after the Friday close. Filled box edges still act as levels.
- Uses TD Sequential counts (his own TradingView rebuild).
- Uses 10-day candles (each candle = 10 trading days).
- Project docs claude/chart_settings.md and claude/pattern_catalog.md hold more detail.

## 4. Setups and patterns he trades (his reads)

Labels: (T) = Taz's read or rule. (a) = tested on data. (c) = hypothesis.

- **Bearish Exhaustion Framework (T):** parabolic extension + RSI divergence at the 80 extreme + MACD rollover on 30m and hourly + confirmed rejection candle at a known level. Entry on a confirmed close below the rejection low.
- **Daily put confirmations (T):** RSI and MACD bearish divergence, close below the 10 SMA, lower highs. He trusts the daily chart over the 5-minute.
- **Gap rules (T):** weekly gaps almost always fill; price stalling above an unfilled weekly gap for weeks = exhaustion. A daily gap up above resistance that fails and fills = put trigger. Trigger can be the touch of last week's close.
- **Hourly 50 tag (T):** hourly 20 SMA crossing below the 50 near the open; trade the tag of the hourly 50 from underneath. Works better when the 50 and 200 are far apart (QQQ ~$3 to $4). The real flush comes once the hourly 50 crosses under the 200.
- **10 EMA crossing below 10 SMA (T):** bearish momentum starting, any timeframe.
- **Two-day triple MA break (T):** close below daily 10, 20 and 50 within two days = dump signal; skip it into a golden cross. Tested (a): no clean put edge; it marks bigger moves both ways.
- **Bar count from the bottom (T):** tops around bar 9 to 11 (daily or weekly).
- **30m gap-back rejection (T):** his "perfect setup." Gap down, rally back into the gap stalling at the 30m 50 SMA without filling, red-green-green-red top, target the 30m 200 SMA.
- **"Flipping the six" (T):** daily TD sell count 6 day (esp. TSLA) that pops and dumps toward the gap fill, often by noon.
- **Two-tap 9 fade (T, partly a):** daily TD 9 is the first tap at the top of a box, pullback, second tap on a 3 to 5 count, dump to the box low in 2 to 8 days. Tested: a lean on his liquid names in the current regime, not proof.
- **Top clues, "ticking time bomb" (T):** many triggers at once. 10/20 cross and 10 EMA/10 SMA cross on the 10-day chart, volume shelf, anchored VWAP, RSI and MACD backtesting from the underside then turning down ("my shit"), MACD plateau. "Cliff diver": 4H sideways for a long time, then breaks under the 50 and 200 together.
- **Intraday turn times (T):** dumps at 11:00; 11 to 12 lunch pullback then small bounce; ~12:50 turn (TOS 4H candle starts at 1:00); ~2:15 to 2:20 turn.
- **Bar-pattern analogs (T):** overlays historical bar sequences onto current charts. Wants this method in the bot.
- **Fed days (T):** market usually gaps up rather than running green candles all day.
